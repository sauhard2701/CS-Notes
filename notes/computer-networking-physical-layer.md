## Communication Modes

Based on the direction in which information is transmitted over a transmission line, communication modes are divided into the following three types:

- Simplex communication: one-way transmission
- Half-duplex communication: two-way alternating transmission
- Full-duplex communication: two-way simultaneous transmission

## Bandpass Modulation

Analog signals are continuous signals, while digital signals are discrete signals. Bandpass modulation converts digital signals into analog signals.
